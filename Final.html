Why was this inferred?
This prototype begins with a simulated assumption that viewing time indicates an interest in Abstract Art.

What might be wrong?
Spending time looking at an artwork does not prove someone likes it. They might be reading its label, comparing it with another work, or simply passing by.

What does Undo do?
It reverses the demonstration's initial simulated inference and takes you to the strongest alternative gallery.

Data transparency
No real AI, eye tracking, or personal data is used. All recommendation values are simulated.
